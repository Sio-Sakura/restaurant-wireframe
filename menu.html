The Menu

Seasonal Italian cuisine, rooted in tradition, shaped by the Midwest

Explore the seasonal Italian menu at Luciana, Chicago's premier fine dining destination. Our kitchen celebrates the art of hand-made pasta, wood-grilled meats, and the finest seasonal ingredients sourced from Midwestern farms and Italian artisan purveyors. Each dish reflects a commitment to honoring Italian culinary tradition while embracing the terroir of the heartland.
