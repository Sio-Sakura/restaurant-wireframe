Our Story

A Chef's Devotion to Italian Craft

Luciana is a fine dining Italian restaurant in Chicago born from Chef Marco De Luca's lifelong reverence for the Italian table. Raised between Naples and Bologna, Marco brings an understanding of Italian cuisine that is deeply traditional yet quietly modern.

Every dish begins with the ingredient. We partner with Midwestern farms and artisan producers to source what is freshest each season, then apply the techniques of Italian cooking to create something both familiar and revelatory — a dialogue between Italian heritage and Chicago terroir.

Marco De Luca

Executive Chef & Founder

"

The Italian kitchen does not seek to impress — it seeks to nourish, to comfort, to remind you of something you have always known. At Luciana, we cook not to astonish, but to honor the ingredient, the season, and the hands that brought them to our table.

— Chef Marco De Luca

The Cellar

An Italian Wine Journey

The wine program at Luciana is a deeply personal collection, assembled over years of travel through Italy's most celebrated and overlooked regions alike. From the nebbiolo of Piedmont to the nerello mascalese of Etna, each bottle is chosen for its ability to illuminate the dish it accompanies.

Our sommelier team crafts pairings that honor the Italian tradition of wine as a companion to food — never a spectacle, always a dialogue. Whether you are drawn to a bold Barolo, a mineral-driven Vermentino, or a rare Aglianico from Campania, Luciana's cellar invites exploration at every level. As Chicago's Italian fine dining destination, we take pride in offering one of the city's most thoughtful Italian wine collections.
