Our Story

Where Italian Heritage
Meets Chicago Soul

Luciana is a fine dining Italian restaurant in Chicago that celebrates the depth and beauty of Italy's culinary traditions. Nestled in the heart of the city, our kitchen draws from generations of Italian cooking — the slow-simmered ragùs of Emilia-Romagna, the coastal brightness of the Amalfi, the rustic elegance of Tuscany — and reinterprets each dish through the lens of the Midwest's finest seasonal ingredients.

Founded by Chef Marco De Luca, Luciana was born from a simple conviction: that Italian cuisine, at its highest expression, is an act of devotion. Every plate is a conversation between old-world technique and new-world possibility, composed with restraint and served with warmth. As one of Chicago's most acclaimed Italian restaurants, we invite guests not merely to dine, but to linger — to rediscover the Italian table as a place of connection, beauty, and quiet celebration.

"Cooking is memory made visible. Each dish carries the weight of the hands that came before."

— Chef Marco De Luca

We believe the finest Italian dining in Chicago begins not with extravagance, but with integrity — sourcing the purest ingredients, honoring time-tested methods, and crafting each moment at the table with intention and grace.

The Cellar

An Italian Wine
Program of Depth

Luciana's wine program is a journey through Italy's most storied regions. Our cellar houses over 400 selections — from the bold Barolos of Piedmont and the structured Brunellos of Montalcino to the mineral-driven whites of Campania and the rare indigenous varietals of Sicily.

Our sommelier team works closely with the kitchen to compose pairings that elevate each course, creating harmonies between plate and glass that feel both surprising and inevitable. Whether you seek a familiar favorite or wish to explore something new, the wine experience at Luciana is designed to deepen your evening at Chicago's finest Italian table.
